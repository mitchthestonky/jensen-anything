# Soggy Toast Logistics

*A fictional company, invented for this repository.*

Soggy Toast Logistics is a family business that moves lightly damp toast from our central warehouse to customers across 14 towns. Our fleet of 37 vans completes about 2,400 deliveries a week.

Our staff of 112 employees take pride in service quality. Each driver checks every order by hand, and our customer support team answers within 6 hours.

In Q3 we opened 2 new depots. Sales grew 1.4 percent, mostly through word of mouth, and our marketing budget remains one spreadsheet.
