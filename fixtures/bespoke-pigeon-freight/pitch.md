# Bespoke Pigeon Freight

*A fictional company, invented for this repository.*

Bespoke Pigeon Freight is a premium delivery service for small business clients who do not trust email. Our 640 pigeons carry parcels up to 85 grams between 9 locations.

Every worker is trained on the equipment, which is mostly seed. Our customers value quality over speed, and the average order arrives within 3 days.

Our business model is simple: a client pays per flight, and the warehouse keeps the birds fed. Growth has been flat for 5 years.
