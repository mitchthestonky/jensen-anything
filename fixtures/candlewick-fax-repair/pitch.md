# Candlewick & Daughters Fax Repair

*A fictional company, invented for this repository.*

Candlewick & Daughters has run the same repair shop since 1974. We fix fax machines for customers in 3 counties, and our team of 8 keeps 1,150 machines in working order.

Our product is a yearly service plan. Each client gets 2 visits, and our inventory of spare parts lives in a spreadsheet and a shoebox.

The market is shrinking by 11 percent a year, but our employees believe in the industry.
