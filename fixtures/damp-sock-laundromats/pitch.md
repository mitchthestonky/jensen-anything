# Damp Sock Laundromats

*A fictional company, invented for this repository.*

Damp Sock Laundromats operates 22 stores, each with 30 machines. Our customers bring their laundry to the nearest branch, and our staff return roughly 94 percent of socks.

The company software is a database from 2009. Each manager reports weekly sales to head office, and our service team handles lost sock claims within 10 days.

We plan to open 4 locations next year, subject to market conditions.
